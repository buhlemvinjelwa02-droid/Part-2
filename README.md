Part-1
Proposal was created for my candle business. The site plan, wire frame and objectives and functionalities of the business are stated. The HTML pages for the business are then created. 
The first HTML page that is created; the homepage, contains a header and footer. 
The header contains the name of the business 'Petrichor Candles', introducing the business to the customer. 
The background colour of the website is set to aqua.
The footer contains the standard phrase '2026 Your website. All Rights Reserved.'The page also has an image of blue candles to advertise the products. 

There are also buttons on the page that the customers can click to navigate to the other HTML pages. 
There are three buttons for the other three HTML pages.
Href has been used to connect the buttons to the HTML pages.
There is a product button where the customer can order the candles.
The button will take the customer to the product HTML page where orders can be made.
The About us button takes the customer to the About us page where the customer can learn about the history of the business, as well as the vision and mission of the business. 
And the Contact Us button takes the customer to the Contact Us page where enquiries can be made by the customers.
The second HTML page, the product page is the page where customers can order candles.
There are pictures of the products (candles) on the page so the customer can see them as they order. On this page they can enter their name, the amount of candles that they want to purchase as well as their address. 
A form is added in order to capture the name and the amount of candles. 
A textarea has been added in order to capture the customer's address. 
The third HTML page is the About us page. This page serves as an educational and informatory page for the customer.
Here the customer can learn the heart and story of the business. 
They learn how the business came about as well as the mission and vision of the business. 
This helps the customer to connect with the business and understand what it stands for.
There is a video on this page of a candle burning. 
The customer can 'light' the candle (by playing the video of the burning candle) as they read this page.
The last HTML page is the Contact Us page. 
This page is intended for the customer to share queries, suggestions and reviews. 
This is intended to be the customer feedback section. 
This page will be very beneficial to the business as the business can use this customer feedback to better their products.

Coding for the typography was done in the CSS as the font-weight was set to 200.
The line-height and letter-spacing were set to normal.
For the buttons, their color was set to black. 
Their box-sizing was set to border-box and its width was set to 20% and their font-size was set to 10 pixels. 
In order to make the buttons more round, their border-radius was set to 8 pixels.
In order to make the cursor hover over the buttons the button:hover function was used.
In the button:hover function, the cursor was set to pointer.
For the header, in the CSS , in order to make the header fill the top of the screen, the display:flex function was used. 
In order to size the images for different screen sizes, the media function is used to to change the size of the pictures.

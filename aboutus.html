About Us :) (light candle as you read)

This business idea was born in August of 2025, during a thunderstorm. Though it was cold and raining, the smell of the earth as it rained was a silver lining.


I absolutely love the smell of the ground when it rains, and after some research, I found out that I'm not the only one.The smell is called Petrichor (hence the name petrichor) and it turns out that it is one of the most universally loved scents.


The only thing is, it can only be smelled when it rains. That was before Petrichor candles. Now anyone can smell the scent whenever they please, even if it is 35 degrees!

Our mission is to provide customers with candles that are of high qaulity while still being affordable for them.Our mission is to build a good relationship with our customers, one of trust and transparency.

Our vision is to become a trusted and respected candle company, one of good qaulity candles and affordable prices.
